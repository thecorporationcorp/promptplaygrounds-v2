Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly are bookmarklets?

Bookmarklets are special bookmarks that run JavaScript code. Instead of going to a website, they perform an action - in this case, injecting a prompt into your AI chat. They're safe, fast, and work in any browser.

Will this work with my AI tool?

Yes! Our bookmarklets work with ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, and any other AI chat interface. If it has a text input, our bookmarklets can inject prompts into it.
