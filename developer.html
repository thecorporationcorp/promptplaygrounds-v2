Submission Guidelines

What we're looking for in prompts

✅ What We Accept

  • High quality: Well-tested, proven prompts that deliver real value
  • Specific: Solves a clear problem or accomplishes a defined task
  • Original: Your own work, not copied from others
  • Clear instructions: Easy to understand and use
  • Professional: Well-written, free of errors
  • All-ages appropriate: No explicit content or harmful instructions

❌ What We Reject

  • Hate speech: No racist, fascist, or discriminatory content
  • Harmful content: Nothing that could harm users (especially minors)
  • Plagiarized: Copied from others without attribution
  • Low effort: Generic, untested, or poorly written prompts
  • Duplicates: Substantially similar to existing prompts
  • Misleading: False claims about what the prompt does
💡 Pro Tip: The best prompts are specific, actionable, and save users significant time or effort. Think: "What repetitive task do I solve brilliantly with AI?"

Developer FAQ

How do payouts work?

Payouts are processed monthly via PayPal. You'll receive 80% of all sales from the previous month. Minimum payout is $10.

Can I submit multiple prompts?

Yes! There's no limit. Submit as many high-quality prompts as you want.

What if my prompt gets rejected?

We'll email you with specific feedback on why it was rejected and suggestions for improvement. You can revise and resubmit.

Can I update my prompt after it's live?

Yes, through your developer dashboard. Updates are reviewed within 24 hours.

Who owns the prompt?

You retain full ownership. We just have a license to distribute it on our platform. You can sell it elsewhere too.
